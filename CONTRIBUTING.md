# Contributing

Issues and discussions are welcome: bug reports, questions, and ideas help.

**Pull requests from outside the organization are not accepted.** They are
closed automatically with a note. This keeps the code, and the licensing of the
bundled engine, under one owner. If you have a fix in mind, describe it in an
issue and we will take it from there.

Please do not include real client data, real bank files, or real QuickBooks
exports in issues. Use the "Copy details" button in the app for a scrubbed
error report.
