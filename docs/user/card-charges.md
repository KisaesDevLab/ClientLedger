# Card charges and credits

Use Record Card Charge for purchases on your business credit card. Open it from **Vendors > Record Card Charge** or press `Ctrl+Shift+C`.

1. Choose **Charge** (a purchase) or **Credit** (a return or refund to the card).
2. Pick the **Card**. Its balance owed shows next to it.
3. Fill in **Purchased from**, the **Date** and the **Amount**.
4. On the lines below, choose the account for each part of the purchase and type its amount. You can add a memo, a customer (tick **Billable** to bill them later) and tags.
5. Press **Save & New** (`Alt+N`) or **Save & Close** (`Alt+S`).

**The lines must add up to the amount.** Below the lines you see how much is left to assign. ClientLedger won't save until they match to the cent.

When you pick a vendor you've bought from before, the accounts from last time are filled in. The amounts stay blank.

A charge raises the balance owed on the card; a credit lowers it. Both show in the card's register right away.

To change or void a charge, find it with **Prev** and **Next**, or press `Ctrl+E` on it in the card's register.
