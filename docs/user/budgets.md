# Budgets

A budget is the amount you plan to earn or spend in each income and expense account, one amount per fiscal period. Compare it with what really happened in **Budget vs. Actual**.

## Making a budget

1. Open **Reports**, pick **Budget vs. Actual**, and click **Edit budgets**.
2. Type the **Fiscal year** (the year it ends in) and a **Name**, then click **New budget**. Each fiscal year has one budget.
3. Type an amount in each period of each account. Use Tab to move across. A row saves when you leave it; the totals update once it's saved.

To fill the budget quickly:

- **Fill from last year** puts last year's actual amounts in each period (calendar months), rounded to the cent. It replaces what is there.
- **Adjust by %** raises or lowers every amount by the percent you type, for example `5` or `-10`.
- **Copy from another budget** copies another year's amounts.

If someone else changed the budget while you were editing, ClientLedger asks you to **Reload**; what you typed stays until you do.

## Budget vs. Actual

Choose your budget under **Budget**. The report runs on that budget's fiscal year, one column group per period:

- **Actual**: what your books show. Click it to see the transactions behind it.
- **Budget**: what you planned.
- **Over budget**: actual minus budget.
- **% of budget**: actual as a share of the budget; a dash when the budget is zero.

All amounts come from your books and your budget; nothing is estimated.
