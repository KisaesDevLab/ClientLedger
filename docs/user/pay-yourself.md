# Pay yourself or put money in

Use this to record money you take out of the business for yourself, or money you put into it. Open it from **Banking > Pay Yourself or Put Money In**.

1. Choose **Pay yourself** or **Put money in**.
2. Pick **Who** (optional for a transfer), the **Bank account**, and how it was paid: **Check** or **Transfer or online**.
3. Check the **Date** and type the **Amount**.
4. **Recorded in** shows the account it goes to. ClientLedger sets this up for your type of business, and you can change it. Your choice is remembered.
5. Press **Save & New** (`Alt+N`) or **Save & Close** (`Alt+S`).

What the account is called depends on your type of business:

- Sole proprietor or single-member LLC: owner draws and owner contributions.
- Partnership: partner draws and partner contributions.
- S corporation or C corporation: shareholder distributions and paid-in capital.

ClientLedger records the money; it doesn't give tax advice. Ask your accountant if you're not sure which to use, or how paying yourself affects your taxes.
