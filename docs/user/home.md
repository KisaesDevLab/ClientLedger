# Home

Home is the screen you start from. Open it any time with `Alt+H` or from the **Window** menu. It shows what you can do next, where your money stands, and what needs your attention.

## The four lanes

Each lane groups four jobs. Click a job, or press the shortcut shown next to it. A job whose screen isn't built yet opens a short "arrives in a later update" window.

- **Money in:** Create Invoice, Receive Payment, Sales Receipt, Make Deposit.
- **Money out:** Enter Bill, Pay Bills, Write Check, Record Card Charge.
- **Banking:** Review Bank Feed, Open Register, Reconcile, Transfer Funds. (Reconciling means checking your books against your bank statement.)
- **Close & report:** Make Journal Entry, Profit & Loss, Send to My Accountant, Set Closing Date.

At the bottom of each lane is one figure: "Clients owe you", "Bills due in 30 days" (the number of days is your setting), "Last reconciled", and "Sales tax owed". A dash means the figure isn't available yet. Point at it to see why. The bills figure shows only when your bills window is 90 days or less. You can change it in Preferences.

## Account balances

**Account balances** lists your bank and card accounts. The **In your books** column is the balance your books report. A card balance can show as a negative number: that is how the books store what you owe.

The **Bank file** column fills in after you import a bank file. That feature comes in a later update.

## Income and expenses chart

The chart ("Income and expenses, last 6 months") shows two bars for each of the last six whole months: income and expenses. Under it you see the month's profit. The profit comes from the Profit & Loss report for that month.

If a month can't be loaded, it shows a dash, not a zero. Choose **Refresh** to try again.

Clicking a month will open that month's report once reports arrive in a later update.

## Needs attention

This list counts things waiting for you, each with a button that takes you there:

- Bank transactions to review (**Review**)
- Reconciliations not finished (**Resume**)
- Invoices past due (**Open aging**)
- Bills overdue or due within your bills window (**Pay bills**)
- Payroll imports not posted yet (**Import**)
- A backup reminder: "Not backed up since ..." or "This company hasn't been backed up yet" (**Back up now**)

When there is nothing to do, it says "Nothing needs attention right now." To-dos, reorder alerts and the weekly health check arrive in a later update.

## Setup checklist

The checklist shows at the top of Home as "Setup checklist 5 of 6 done". It has six steps:

1. Add your company name and address
2. Add a bank account
3. Enter your first transaction
4. Import a bank file
5. Set up email for invoices
6. Make your first backup

Steps tick by themselves when ClientLedger sees you have done them. You can't tick them by hand. The full list opens by itself while fewer than half the steps are done. Use the checklist link to show or hide it. Each step has a **Start** button.

Choose **Hide checklist** to hide it for this company. Steps still tick as you finish them. The checklist also goes away once everything is done.

## Refresh

**Refresh** reads the balances and counts again. Use it after you change something in another window, or when a card says "Couldn't load". Each failed card also has **Try again** and **Copy details**. Copy details copies a scrubbed error note you can paste into a bug report. It has no amounts or names.
