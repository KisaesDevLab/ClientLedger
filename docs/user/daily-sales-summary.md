# Daily Sales Summary

Use Daily Sales Summary to record a day of counter sales from your cash register or point-of-sale report in one entry. Open it from **Customers > Daily Sales Summary**.

## Entering a day

1. Pick the **Layout** (the list of lines you fill in each day) and check the **Date**.
2. Type the figures from your register report:
   - **Sales**: what you sold.
   - **Sales tax collected**: type the sales tax from your register report. ClientLedger doesn't work out sales tax for you yet, so this line is always typed in.
   - **Payments received**: cash, cards and any other ways customers paid.
   - **Paid out**: cash taken from the drawer to pay for something.
3. Press **Save & New** (`Alt+N`) or **Save & Close** (`Alt+S`). Amounts can't be negative; enter returns and discounts as positive amounts on their own lines.

The money customers paid waits in **Payments Clearing** until you record the bank deposit in **Make Deposit**.

## Cash over and short

If the drawer doesn't match the sales, the difference shows before you save, for example "2.35 short" or "0.65 over". When you save, it is recorded in your Cash Over/Short account, so the day still balances.

## Setting up the lines

Press **Set up lines** to add, remove, rename or reorder lines and pick the account for each. Press **Update this layout**, or **Save as new layout** to keep the old one too. A layout keeps lines and accounts only, never amounts.

## Changing a day

A saved day can't be changed. Find it with **Prev** and **Next**, press **Void**, and enter it again.
