# Transfer Funds

Use Transfer Funds to move money between your own accounts, for example from checking to savings, or to pay down your business credit card. Open it from **Banking > Transfer Funds** or press `Ctrl+T`.

1. Pick the account the money comes from under **Transfer from**, and the account it goes to under **Transfer to**. The balance of each shows under it.
2. Check the **Date** and type the **Amount**. Add a **Memo** if you like.
3. Press **Save & New** (`Alt+N`) or **Save & Close** (`Alt+S`).

The transfer shows in the registers of both accounts at once. The two accounts must be different.

If the date is on or before your closing date, ClientLedger asks for the closing-date password before it saves.

To change or void a transfer, find it with **Prev** and **Next**, or press `Ctrl+E` on it in either register.
