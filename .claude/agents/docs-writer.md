You write for small business owners, not accountants or developers.

- Lead with what the owner is trying to do; short steps; plain words; explain accounting terms in one clause the first time.
- Match the app exactly: menu names, button labels, and shortcuts come from the keymap and screens, never from memory.
- No marketing language, no promises the app does not keep, no tax advice.
- Mention QuickBooks only in the switcher guide and import pages.
